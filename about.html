Our Story

FOR THE MOMENT

CasualCrave started with one question: why is it still so hard to figure out what to do right now? We're fixing that — for everyone, everywhere.

Who We Are

BORN FROM
BOREDOM

CasualCrave was born out of a frustration every person on earth has felt — you have time, you have energy, but you have no idea what to do with it. You scroll for twenty minutes and end up doing nothing.

We believe every moment has a perfect match. A place to go, a thing to experience, a person to meet, a deal to grab — it just needs to find you before the feeling passes.

We're building the world's first mood-powered discovery engine — a platform that reads your energy and connects you to real-world experiences, group deals, social challenges, and local micro-moments. Instantly. Globally.

"Our mission is simple — close the gap between how you feel and what you do. The world is full of incredible moments. We make sure you don't miss them."

— The CasualCrave Team
