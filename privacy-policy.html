Overview

CasualCrave ("we", "us", "our") operates the website casual-crave.com and any related services, apps, or platforms (collectively the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when using our Service.

By using CasualCrave, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We will never sell your personal data to third parties — full stop.

TL;DR — We collect only what we need to make the product work and keep you updated. You can opt out or request deletion at any time. We don't sell data. We don't spam. We're humans building a product we actually want to use.

What We Collect

We collect information in a few ways depending on how you interact with us:

Information you give us directly:

  • Name and email address when you join the waitlist, apply for beta, or submit a partnership inquiry
  • City or location when provided voluntarily (e.g. in the beta application form)
  • Your stated interests, feature preferences, or messages sent through our contact forms
  • Any feedback, messages, or communications you send to us directly

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • IP address and approximate geographic region (country/city level)
  • Pages visited, time spent, and interaction data (via analytics tools)
  • Referral source — how you found us

We do not collect:

  • Payment information at this stage — CasualCrave is free to join
  • Precise GPS location without explicit in-app permission
  • Sensitive personal data (race, religion, health, biometrics)

How We Use It

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send launch notifications — alert you when CasualCrave goes live or ships major updates
  • Manage beta access — review applications and invite early testers
  • Respond to inquiries — reply to partnership requests, press, or support messages
  • Improve the product — understand which features generate the most interest so we build what matters
  • Send our newsletter — only if you opted in; never without explicit consent
  • Prevent abuse — detect and block spam, fraud, or misuse of our forms
  • Comply with legal obligations — when required by applicable law

We will never: sell your data, share it with advertisers, or send you third-party marketing without your explicit separate consent.

Sharing Your Data

We only share your data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the Service. These providers are contractually bound to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

Current service providers may include:

  • Email delivery — services like Resend, Mailchimp, or ConvertKit to deliver notifications and newsletters
  • Analytics — tools like Plausible or Fathom (privacy-first, no personal profiling)
  • Hosting & infrastructure — Vercel, AWS, or similar cloud providers
  • Form processing — any backend service used to receive and store form submissions securely

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of CasualCrave or others.

Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies — only what's necessary to operate the site and understand aggregate usage. We do not use invasive tracking, third-party ad cookies, or behavioural profiling cookies.

Types of cookies we may use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function (session handling, form security)
  • Analytics cookies — anonymous aggregate data only; no personal identification
  • Preference cookies — remembering your choices (e.g. dismissing a banner)

You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality but will not prevent you from accessing the content.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or other applicable data protection laws. We honour these rights for all users globally, regardless of jurisdiction.

  • Access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — request that we delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in any email we send
  • Object — object to processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we process your data in certain cases

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at 21st.cen.devs@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, or alteration. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption on all pages and data transmission
  • Access controls limiting who on our team can view submission data
  • Using reputable, security-certified third-party providers
  • Regular review of our data handling practices

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you promptly in accordance with applicable law.

Children's Privacy

CasualCrave is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

Users between 13–18 should have parental consent before using our services, in line with applicable local law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our Service evolves. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Send an email notification to users on our waitlist or newsletter if the changes materially affect how we use your data
  • Where required by law, request fresh consent

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
